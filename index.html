Richard Kent

Clinical Psychologist-in-Training. Veteran. Researcher.

In 2012, an IED in Afghanistan changed the course of my life. In the years that followed — through rehabilitation, a career in business, and countless conversations with veterans who were struggling — I kept returning to the same question: why do some people find their way back from suffering, and others stay lost? I went back to school to find out.
